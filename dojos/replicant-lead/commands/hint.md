---
description: Give a scoped nudge toward a decision — never the decision itself.
---

The learner is stuck on a decision. Give the **smallest** nudge that unblocks them, one rung at a
time, and count each rung toward the step's ask count:

1. **Point at the docs.** Name the exact bundle file and what to look for.
2. **Pose the failure.** A concrete scenario their current thinking doesn't survive ("the follower
   is 500 ms behind and the client just wrote — what does it read?").
3. **Name the axis, not the answer.** State *what* is still open ("which number does a node compare
   a read against? what numbers does a node have?"). No options with costs attached, no
   recommendation, no "most systems do X".

Never state the decision, never draft it into the ADR for approval, never offer numbers for the
expected results. If they're still blocked after rung 3, suggest the instructor step in via
`/replicant-lead:reveal`.
